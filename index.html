The places

Every Sandbite begins with a place: its mornings, its ingredients, its landscape, the people who call it home and the flavours that bring it back to life. We turn those stories into bars you can carry with you.

A real taste of somewhere worth remembering.

Our story

He moved across the world. Got bit.

Our founder’s story is simple: he moved across the world and got bit by the places, people and cultures that stayed with him.

This world is full of beautiful cultures and places many people may never get to see. We designed Sandbite to carry the taste and energy of the people, cultures and experiences behind each location.

With each bar, we aim to educate, share and pass on teachings from nature-connected cultures that still thrive today.

What we stand for

Sourced from the place. Designed to give back.

The land

Ocean and earth are the source and the responsibility. Ingredients I can trace to a grower, bought from the coast a bar is named after, as little packaging as I can get away with, and nothing I would not want washing up on my own beach. If a choice is cheaper but worse for the place, it is not a Sandbite choice.

The culture

Represented, not borrowed. A place is its people first: the growers, fishermen, shapers and crews who already live the way this brand talks about. They get named, paid and photographed as themselves, and each bar carries the name of a break I have actually surfed rather than a country I have only visited.
