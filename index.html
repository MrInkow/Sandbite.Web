01
Why we bothered

Snack bars became too boring.

The category

A wall of safe rectangles

Clean claims, flat textures, products people tolerate because they are supposed to be good for them. Somewhere in there the snack stopped being a treat and became an assignment.

Our position

Adults are still big kids

People still like opening things, discovering something, making a mess and having a product with a point of view. Growing up is not a reason to lose that.

The rule

Taste first, not taste only. We will not make something that tastes bad and ask you to accept it because it is healthy. It has to satisfy the craving first and earn the right to talk about ingredients second.

02
The format

Tear. Push. Squeeze. Bite.

One tear opens the crumble section and the jam section together. Push the crumble up, squeeze the jam on top, and eat it however you want. Ignore all of that and it still works as a snack.

Step 01Tear

One pull opens both sections at once.

Step 02Push

The crumble slides up. No clean table needed.

Step 03Squeeze

As much as you want, or none at all.

Step 04Bite

Crumbs are the point. Mess should be intentional.

Built for

Dirty hands. You should be able to open it and eat it on a tailgate, a deck or a scaffold plank without needing somewhere clean to put it down.

Flavour 01

Coffee

Real coffee in the crumble, not a coffee flavouring. Nuts, oats, dates and honey underneath it. It is a sweet break that happens to keep you going, rather than a supplement that happens to be sweet.

04
Who it's for

For people who haven't grown boring.

The people who are already using their hands all day. End of a shift, halfway up a job, off the boat, between sessions. The break is short and it should be worth something.

The moment

The pause inside the work

You sit down, look back at what you have just done, and have five minutes. That is the whole occasion. We are not trying to own the job — just the break in the middle of it.

The standard

Holds up in a bag

It is built to crumble, which is the point, but it still has to survive a toolbag, a glovebox and a hot van. Mess should be intentional. Failure should not.

The promise

No lecture attached

Recognisable ingredients and useful energy, said plainly. We are not going to pretend this is medicine or put a performance claim on it we cannot back up.
