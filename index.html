01 — Why we bothered

Snack bars
became too boring.

A wall of safe rectangles and clean claims. Somewhere in there the snack stopped being a treat and turned into an assignment. Adults are still big kids — growing up is not a reason to lose that.

02
The format

Tear. Push. Squeeze. Bite.

One tear opens the crumble and the jam together. Push it up, squeeze the jam on top, eat it however you want. Ignore all of that and it still works as a snack.

Step 01Tear

One pull opens both sections at once.

Step 02Push

It slides up. No clean table needed.

Step 03Squeeze

As much as you want, or none.

Step 04Bite

Crumbs are the point.

Built for dirty handsNo clean table required

Open it and eat it on a tailgate, a deck or a scaffold plank. Mess should be intentional — failure should not.

03 — Flavour 01

Coffee

Real coffee through the crumble, not a coffee flavouring. Nuts, oats, dates and honey underneath it. A sweet break that happens to keep you going, rather than a supplement that happens to be sweet.
