What we stand for

Sourced from the place. Designed to give back.

The land

Ocean and earth are the source and the responsibility. The standard we are building toward: ingredients we can trace to a grower, bought from the coast a bar is named after. Today that means as little packaging as we can get away with, and nothing I would not want washing up on our beaches. If a choice is cheaper but worse for the place, it is not a Sandbite choice.

The culture

Represented, not borrowed. A place is its people first: the growers, makers, first responders and crews who already live the way we stand for. Belonging isn’t about where you’re from. It’s about showing up, doing the work, and leaving the place better than you found it.

Our story

Our team got bit.

Our founder’s story is simple: moved across the world and got bit by the places, people and cultures.

Knowing the privilege experienced, we designed Sandbite to carry the taste and energy that was gracefully shared with us over the years.

With each bar, we aim to discover, learn from, and maintain the places that mark us.
