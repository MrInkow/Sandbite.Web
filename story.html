Where it came from

Two years after leaving.

Sandbite started two years after leaving Hawaii and moving to Portugal, looking for some of the beauty I'd left behind. I was bored of work, bored of doing nothing, and I knew I wanted to learn something, build something that meant anything, and give something back.

Grandma Sara's coffee beans

Chocolate-covered coffee beans, handed to me on the Big Island. The thought was simple: we don't really eat coffee over here, and I reckoned I could make it taste amazing.

The date cake from KTA

After a few attempts a second memory turned up in the recipe: the date cake from KTA in Waimea. That's where the crumble came from.

Wanting to watch someone eat it

I was working on software and felt disconnected from the point of the job. Food fixed that. You cook it, you hand it to someone, you watch them eat it, and you find out immediately whether they want another. There's nowhere to hide in that.

Where it is now

Made by hand.

The bombeiros in Ericeira were among the first to try it and say something honest about it.

It's for the break: working with your hands, out exploring, or getting through a long day indoors. Something delicious to stop for.
