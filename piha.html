Piha

Seven years, longer than I have lived anywhere. The place that teaches the meaning of respect.

Piha is black sand and a rip that teaches you quickly, and it is the closest thing I have to a second home break.

Manuka honey instead of regular, and a friendship that taught me what it actually means to respect a culture rather than borrow from it. That lesson is why this bar carries the name of the break and not a name that is not mine to use.
